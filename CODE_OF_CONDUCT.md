# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the HubSight Admin SDK project a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and considerate;
- assuming good intent while remaining open to correction;
- giving and receiving constructive technical feedback;
- focusing on the issue, implementation, or documentation rather than the person;
- protecting private information and credentials;
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberately exposing credentials, security-sensitive data, or private URLs;
- trolling, insulting, or sustained disruptive behavior;
- any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering project.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct and may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
unacceptable behavior.

## Reporting

Please do not report security vulnerabilities in a public issue. For conduct
concerns, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository's
GitHub contact mechanisms and include relevant links, context, and any available
supporting information. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
